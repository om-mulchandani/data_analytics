# What Is a Website?

A **website** is a collection of related web pages and resources, usually identified by a shared domain name and accessed over the internet with a web browser. A website can provide information, services, communication, entertainment, or tools.

Websites may be **static**, serving mostly prebuilt content, or **dynamic**, generating or changing content in response to data or user actions. Many sites combine both approaches.

There is no single exhaustive list of website types. Sites can serve more than one purpose; the categories below are common examples.

## Common Types of Websites

- **Personal or portfolio website** — Presents an individual's biography, work, skills, or projects.
- **Business or corporate website** — Describes an organization, its products or services, and contact information.
- **E-commerce website** — Lets users browse and buy products or services online.
- **Blog** — Publishes regularly updated articles, opinions, or guides, often in reverse chronological order.
- **News or magazine website** — Publishes current events, reports, features, or other editorial content.
- **Educational website** — Provides courses, lessons, learning resources, or academic information.
- **Government website** — Shares official information and may provide public services or forms.
- **Nonprofit or charity website** — Explains a cause, shares activities, and may accept donations or volunteers.
- **Social networking website** — Helps users create profiles, connect, and share content or messages.
- **Forum or community website** — Lets members discuss topics and ask or answer questions.
- **Wiki or knowledge base** — Organizes reference information, often with pages that multiple contributors can edit.
- **Entertainment or media website** — Offers games, videos, music, or other media.
- **Streaming website** — Delivers audio or video for playback over the internet.
- **Web application** — Provides interactive tools or services in a browser, such as email, online banking, or project management.
- **Portal** — Brings together links, information, or services from multiple sources in one place.
- **Search engine** — Helps users find web pages and other online information.
- **Directory or listing website** — Organizes entries such as businesses, services, jobs, or properties so users can search or browse them.
- **Booking or reservation website** — Supports reservations for travel, events, appointments, or accommodation.
- **Landing page** — A focused page designed to explain or promote one offer, campaign, or action.

**In brief:** A website is an online collection of related pages or services; its type is usually named after its main purpose.

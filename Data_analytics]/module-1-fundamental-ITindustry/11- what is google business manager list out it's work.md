# What Is Google Business Manager?

“**Google Business Manager**” is often used informally to mean the tools for managing a business's presence on Google. Google's current product name is **Google Business Profile**; it was previously called **Google My Business**. Businesses manage eligible profiles through Google Search and Google Maps using a Google Account.

A **Business Profile** is a business listing that can appear in Google Search and Maps. It is especially useful for businesses that serve customers in person or travel to serve them. It is not the same as **Google Ads**, which is Google's advertising platform.

## What You Can Do With a Google Business Profile

- **Create or claim a profile** — Add a business or request control of an existing listing, then complete Google's verification process when required.
- **Manage business information** — Add or update the business name, category, address or service area, phone number, website, and opening hours.
- **Show products and services** — Describe what the business offers; available features can vary by business type and region.
- **Add photos and updates** — Share images, announcements, offers, or other profile updates where those features are available.
- **Interact with customers** — Reply to reviews and, where available, answer customer questions or messages.
- **Help customers find and contact the business** — A profile can appear in Search and Maps with details such as directions, hours, and contact options.
- **Review performance information** — See available insights about how people find and interact with the profile, such as searches, calls, or direction requests.
- **Manage access** — Add owners or managers so authorized people can help maintain the profile.
- **Keep details current** — Update hours for holidays, temporary closures, or other changes.

## Important Points

- A Business Profile is generally free to create and manage, but Google may change features and eligibility requirements.
- Eligibility and available features depend on Google's policies, the business type, and the region. A profile is generally intended for businesses that interact with customers in person; online-only businesses may not qualify.
- The business should use accurate information and follow Google's guidelines. Verification does not guarantee a particular position in Search or Maps.
- A profile can improve a business's visibility, but it does not replace a website and does not guarantee customers or rankings.

**In brief:** Google Business Profile helps eligible businesses manage how their information appears in Google Search and Maps and interact with customers there.

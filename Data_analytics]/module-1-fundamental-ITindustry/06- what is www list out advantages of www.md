# What Is the WWW?

**WWW** stands for the **World Wide Web**. It is a system of interlinked web pages and other online resources that people access over the internet using a web browser.

Web resources are identified by URLs, connected through hyperlinks, and commonly transferred using **HTTP** or **HTTPS**. Websites may contain text, images, audio, video, and interactive features.

The **World Wide Web is not the same as the internet**. The internet is the global network that connects computers and other devices. The Web is one service that runs on that network; email and file transfer are other internet services.

## Advantages of the World Wide Web

- **Easy access to information** — Find educational, news, research, and reference materials from many sources.
- **Global communication** — Share information and connect with people around the world through websites, forums, and web-based communication tools.
- **Learning opportunities** — Use online courses, tutorials, digital libraries, and other educational resources.
- **Convenient services** — Access banking, shopping, government services, bookings, and other tools online.
- **Supports businesses** — Organizations can present products and services, reach customers, and provide support through websites.
- **Multimedia content** — Combine text, images, audio, video, and interactive content in one resource.
- **Hyperlinks connect resources** — Move between related pages and sources with a click or tap.
- **Collaboration and sharing** — Create, publish, and work on content with others, often from different locations.
- **Available across devices** — Many websites work on computers, phones, and tablets with an internet connection.
- **Fast distribution and updates** — Publishers can make new or corrected information available without printing and redistributing physical copies.

## Things to Keep in Mind

Web content can be inaccurate, outdated, or misleading, and not all services are safe or private. Check sources carefully and use secure websites when sharing sensitive information.
